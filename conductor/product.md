# Product Definition: Kaladhar Studio

**Tagline:** AI-Powered Autonomous Video Reel Generation Engine for Ethnic Indian Wear Boutiques

## 1. Vision & Overview
Kaladhar is an autonomous studio workstation tailored for Indian ethnic wear businesses—specifically handloom saree boutiques, bridal lehenga ateliers, kurtis designers, and ethnic D2C fashion labels.

Small and medium-sized boutique owners capture raw, authentic smartphone photoshoots of their garments, models, and handloom fabrics. However, converting these raw assets into high-converting, professional, rhythm-synced 9:16 vertical video reels for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and WhatsApp Status currently requires costly video editors, complicated desktop software, or hours of manual editing.

Kaladhar solves this by turning raw, unedited photoshoot takes into studio-grade vertical reels in minutes. It intelligently analyzes fabric textures, detects key ethnic wear focal points (such as the pallu drape, zari borders, motifs, and pleats), synchronizes transitions to regional musical beats, and provides a zero-friction browser workstation with direct 1-click MP4 export.

## 2. Target Audience & Personas
1. **Ethnic Boutique Owners & Handloom Weavers**:
   - Need rapid, high-volume video content for daily Instagram drops.
   - Non-technical, operating primarily on mobile and web browsers.
   - Require accurate representation of fabric sheen, embroidery, and true color tone.
2. **Instagram D2C Fashion Brands**:
   - Need high-retention 9:16 vertical reels with synchronized transitions and captivating pacing.
   - Want customizable branding, typography, price tags, and collection titles.
3. **Independent Content Creators & Stylists**:
   - Want rapid assembly of multi-angle photoshoot clips into polished lookbooks.

---

## 3. Core Capabilities & Feature Sets

### A. Intelligent Ethnic Wear Frame Reframing & Computer Vision
- **Smart 9:16 Auto-Reframing**: Automatically reframes horizontal and standard takes to vertical (1080x1920) while dynamically tracking model movements and garments.
- **Garment Detail & Texture Salience**: Detects high-detail fabric zones (Banarasi brocade, Kanjeevaram zari borders, intricate chikankari, and lehenga embroidery) to produce cinematic zoom-in accents.
- **Quality & Blur Filtering**: Filters out shaky, out-of-focus, or low-lighting frames before sequencing.

### B. Beat-Synced Rhythm & Multi-Track Pacing
- **Audio Onset & Transient Detection**: Identifies downbeats, percussive drops, and tempo shifts in regional and ambient ethnic background scores.
- **Cut-on-the-Beat Sequencing**: Automatically aligns clip cuts and camera speed ramps with the rhythm for maximum social media engagement.

### C. Creator Studio & Non-Destructive Timeline Editor
- **Interactive Multi-Clip Timeline**: Inspect, re-order, trim, and preview individual clips.
- **Real-Time 9:16 Playback**: Fluid in-browser preview matching mobile dimensions.
- **Boutique Identity & Overlays**: Auto-inserts boutique watermark, collection title bumper, and product pricing tags.

### D. Production-Grade Export ( Cloud Architecture)
- **Zero Cloud Storage Overhead**: Operates with a client-first privacy model where projects and reels are cached locally in the user browser (localStorage) and rendered media files download directly to device storage.
- **Broadcast-Standard Encoding**: Produces 1080x1920 H.264 High Profile Level 4.2 MP4s with yuv420p color space, constant frame rate (30 fps), and faststart headers, guaranteeing zero playback stutter or black bars on Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp.

---

## 4. Success Metrics & Goals
- **Time to Reel**: Under 60 seconds from upload to completed draft.
- **Video Fidelity**: 100% native Instagram Reels compatibility without recompression artifacts.
- **Zero Cost Barrier**:  recurring database or cloud hosting storage costs for the creator workspace.
